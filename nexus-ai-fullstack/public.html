Understand the risks before trading

Ethereum and other digital assets are volatile. Automated trading can result in partial or total loss of allocated capital. Fees, slippage, network congestion, strategy failures, and technical issues can affect outcomes. Historical or simulated performance does not guarantee future results. Never invest funds you cannot afford to lose.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Does NexusAI guarantee profits?

No. No AI model or automated strategy can guarantee trading profits or prevent losses.

Does creating an account enable trading?

No. Account registration and wallet authorization are separate. The included wallet connection is read-only; live trading is not enabled.

Which wallets or exchanges are supported?

The robot can connect to an Ethereum wallet extension injected into your browser to display your public account and ETH balance. It does not sign or send transactions.

Can I stop the trading robot?

The included terminal is a paper-market simulator and does not submit blockchain transactions.

How are account details protected?

The production application should use server-side authentication, secure sessions, restricted access, and appropriate data-retention practices. This visual preview is not a production authentication service.
